# 00 — Safety, scope and legal boundaries

## What this lab does

This is a software-only learning environment. It teaches:
- mobile-network architecture and terminology;
- subscriber identity concepts using fabricated identities;
- 5G registration and PDU-session concepts;
- how simulated UE/gNB components interact with a private core;
- analysis of your own lab signalling captures;
- cellular positioning mathematics using synthetic coordinates and measurements;
- authorization, privacy and audit requirements in location services.

## What this lab does not do

It does not:
- find a real person's live location from a phone number;
- query a mobile operator's private subscriber databases;
- access SS7, Diameter or carrier signalling networks;
- intercept calls, SMS or third-party traffic;
- bypass SIM authentication or network access controls;
- create a rogue base station or transmit cellular RF;
- provide a location of a real UE from UERANSIM.

UERANSIM is a simulator. Its simulated UE and gNB do not create a physical radio environment or genuine RF positioning measurements.

## Safe operating rules

1. Use only synthetic subscriber identities and keys generated for this lab.
2. Bind databases and management interfaces to loopback unless there is a documented, secured need otherwise.
3. Do not bridge the lab to a commercial operator network.
4. Do not use real SIM credentials, IMSIs, MSISDNs, IMEIs or subscriber records.
5. Capture only traffic generated by your own lab.
6. Keep a record of configuration changes and test outcomes.
7. Delete lab secrets when the lab is decommissioned.

## Location data is sensitive

Real subscriber location is protected information. Access is controlled by operators and applicable law. Any real-world location service must have an appropriate legal basis, authorization, purpose limitation, access controls, retention policy and audit trail.
